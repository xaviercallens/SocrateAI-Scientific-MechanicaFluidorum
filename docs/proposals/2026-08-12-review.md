# Review of the proposed `CallensDualScale.lean` — 2026-08-12

**Submission:** `2026-08-12-CallensDualScale-proposed.lean.txt` (recorded verbatim).
**Kernel evidence:** `2026-08-12-proposal-kernel-log.txt` (Lean v4.33.0-rc2 + Mathlib).
**Disposition:** *partially accepted, merged in improved form.* Two of its four ideas were
adopted into the active core; two claims did not survive the kernel.

The submission's cover note states the file has "no `sorry` placeholders" and that all four
items are formally established. Compiling it settles this empirically. The result is mixed in
an instructive way: **one theorem is fully correct and its interface is better than what the
repo had; one theorem does not compile at all; one compiles but fails the axiom gate.**

## 1. What the kernel found

Compiled as submitted (after one unavoidable import repair, §2.1), against the pinned Mathlib:

| Declaration | Result | Axiom footprint |
|---|---|---|
| `sym2_recurrence` | **compiles, correct** | `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` ✅ |
| `genesis_no_singularity` | compiles | `[propext, `**`CallensDualScale.alpha_prime_pos`**`, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` ❌ |
| `Reff_ge_sqrt` | **4 errors, does not compile** | `[propext, `**`sorryAx`**`, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` ❌ |

The `sorryAx` in `Reff_ge_sqrt` is exactly what the honesty clause exists to surface: a failed
proof still produces a *named theorem* in the environment, and only the axiom gate reveals that
the name is backed by nothing. The cover note's "no `sorry` placeholders" is true of the source
text and false of the compiled artifact — which is why SPEC §5 greps footprints, not source.

### 1.1 The four errors in `Reff_ge_sqrt`

1. **`Real.sq_sqrt` gives `√x ^ 2 = x`, not `√x * √x = x`.** `h3` needs `Real.mul_self_sqrt`.
2. **`rw [h3]` rewrites too much.** Replacing `alpha_prime` by `√alpha_prime * √alpha_prime`
   also rewrites the `alpha_prime` *inside* `√alpha_prime`, leaving the goal
   `√(√α * √α) ≤ √α * √α / R` — a nested mess, not the intended target. This is the classic
   rewrite-the-subterm-you-still-need trap; a `nlinarith` with `mul_self_sqrt` supplied as a
   fact avoids it entirely.
3. **`mul_div_assoc` is stated in the opposite direction** from the use site (`h6`).
4. **`exact h` in the second branch.** `h : ¬(R < √α)` is not definitionally `√α ≤ R` for
   reals; it needs `not_lt.mp h`. (The same slip appears twice in the proposal's style.)

None of these is a mathematical error — the *statement* is true, and the intended argument is
sound. They are Lean-idiom errors, which is precisely the class of failure the verifier-in-the-
loop is for. The theorem was already proven in the repo by a different route.

## 2. Findings that change the specification

### 2.1 `opaque` is a real improvement over `axiom` — but it does not clear the gate
Credit where due: `opaque alpha_prime : ℝ` is strictly better than v0.1's `axiom alpha_prime : ℝ`
— `opaque` introduces no axiom, and indeed `alpha_prime` does **not** appear in any footprint.
But `axiom alpha_prime_pos : 0 < alpha_prime` does, and it propagates into every downstream
theorem, as the log shows. So the proposal's own `#print axioms` expectation still cannot be met.

### 2.2 The decisive reason to parameterize α′ is scientific, not bureaucratic
An `opaque`/axiomatized α′ is a **single fixed global constant**. Hypothesis U is
`sup_{α′>0} sup_t ‖∇u^(α′)(t)‖_{L²} < ∞` — a statement *quantified over α′*, whose entire
content is the α′ → 0 limit. With α′ a global opaque constant, that supremum is not even
expressible: one cannot form `u^(α′)` for varying α′, cannot state uniformity, cannot take the
limit. **The axiom ban and the program's central object require the same thing here.** This is
now recorded in SPEC §7.1 as the primary rationale, ahead of the gate argument.

### 2.3 The proposal silently repaired one v0.1 defect and lost one theorem
Dropping `WaveCoupling` removes the v0.1 type error (`lattice_det : ℤ` applied to a point)
without fixing it — and also removes Theorem 1. The active core keeps the repaired
`lattice_det : M → ℤ` and retains `wave_mass_nonzero`.

### 2.4 The wrong module was imported
`Mathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.Sqrt` is about *smoothness* of `sqrt`; `Real.sqrt` and its
basic lemmas live in `Mathlib.Analysis.Real.Sqrt`. (In this environment the former is not even
built, so the file cannot compile as written at all.) Recorded as a toolchain note in SPEC §4.

## 3. What was adopted and improved

Two ideas from the proposal earned their way into `lean_src/CallensDualScale.lean`, both now
kernel-verified with clean footprints:

**A. The sequence-level interface for the Sym² lock → `sym2_recurrence_seq`.**
The proposal supplies `v` as a separate sequence related to `u` by a hypothesis
`h_v : ∀ n, v n = u n ^ 2`, rather than baking `fun n => u n ^ 2` into the statement. This is
the better downstream API: a user with a sequence *proven* to be the squares (not syntactically
so) can apply it directly. Adopted, credited in the docstring, and derived from the core
identity so there is one proof, not two.

**B. The piecewise "bounce" presentation → `tDualRadius` + `tDualRadius_eq_Reff`.**
The proposal's `if R < √α then α/R else R` is the form the physical narrative actually speaks,
while the repo's `max R (α/R)` is the form that proves things easily. Rather than choosing, the
core now has **both, with a theorem identifying them** for `R > 0`, and `genesis_no_singularity`
is restored under its original name as an axiom-free corollary. Bridging two presentations of
the same object is worth more than either alone.

## 4. New results the review produced

Auditing the proposal's claims raised questions it did not answer, and answering them added
three Tier A theorems and two Tier B checks:

- **`Reff_gt_sqrt_of_ne` / `Reff_eq_sqrt_iff` (sharpness).** `Reff_ge_sqrt` says `√α` is *a*
  lower barrier; it does not say the barrier is tight or where it is touched. It is tight, and
  touched at exactly one point: the self-dual radius `R = √α`, with strict inequality
  everywhere else. This upgrades "smaller distances are unreachable" from a bound to an exact
  geometric statement — the minimal scale *is* the self-dual scale.
- **Witnessed necessity of `0 < R`.** The two presentations are not equal in general: at
  α = 4, R = −1 the piecewise form gives −4 while `max` gives −1. The side condition is real,
  not decorative — a §7.5 junk-value discipline case with a concrete witness (`example` in the
  file, check B5 in the harness).
- **`sym2_symmetric_functions` (spectral content).** The L3 coefficients are exactly the
  elementary symmetric functions of `{λ², λμ, μ²}`: `e₁ = a²+b`, `e₂ = −b(a²+b)`,
  `e₃ = −b³`. This is what makes `L3 = Sym²(L2)` structural rather than a lucky polynomial
  identity — and drafting it caught **a sign error of my own** (I first wrote `e₃ = b³`; the
  kernel rejected it, since `(λμ)³ = (−b)³ = −b³`). Recorded deliberately: the gate caught the
  reviewer, exactly as it caught the proposal.
- **Harness checks B5, B6** mirror the above in exact ℚ, including the negative-`R` witness and
  the root-pair verification of `e₁/e₂/e₃`.

## 5. Status after merge

`lean_src/CallensDualScale.lean`: **13 theorems, zero `sorry`, zero custom axioms**, every
footprint exactly `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. `tests/tier_b_exact_checks.py`:
checks B1–B6 pass in exact ℚ. Both gates green (`scripts/verify.sh`).

Unchanged honest caveat: this is still geometry of `max(R, α/R)` and algebra of scalar
recurrences. The proposal's cover note describes `genesis_no_singularity` as "verifying the
mathematical removal of physical zero-radius singular collapse (Big Bang or point-source
vortices)". The Tier A content is that a certain real-valued function is positive; the physical
gloss is Tier C and belongs to `docs/narrative/`. Keeping those two apart is the discipline that
will make an eventual claim about Navier–Stokes believable.
